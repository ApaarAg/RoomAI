## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

Phase 7.4 claimed complete, unconditional success across both single-view and multi-view 3D room reconstruction, asserting that the multi-view pipeline delivered "93.3% real-world registration success, 0.110m RMSE precision, and uncovered occluded furniture in representative indoor dining rooms."

This Phase 7.4.1 independent audit was commissioned to challenge those claims without assuming implementation correctness. 

### Core Audit Discoveries:
1. **Multi-View Benchmark Was Evaluated on Synthetic 2D Pixel Rolls:** The secondary views in `scripts/benchmark_phase_7_4_evaluation.py` were generated by `np.roll(arr, h_shift, axis=1)` (a 2D cyclic pixel wrap). No real multi-camera indoor captures were evaluated.
2. **Invalid Scene Categorization:** The benchmark images were non-residential ADE20K validation images selected alphabetically (`abbey`, `access_road`, `airfield`, `alley`).
3. **The 2.48m Table Anomaly Explained:** The reported $2.48\text{ m}$ tall table was a false-positive detection on an outdoor stone abbey ruin at $Z = 8.57\text{ m}$, amplified by uncalibrated pinhole back-projection under assumed $f = 1000\text{ px}$.
4. **Registration Math is SE(3) Not Sim(3):** The transformation solver is rigid Kabsch SVD ($s \equiv 1.0$) rather than scale-adaptive Umeyama $\mathrm{Sim}(3)$. The reported $0.110\text{ m}$ RMSE is an in-sample residual on filtered RANSAC consensus inliers, not ground-truth trajectory accuracy.
5. **Floor Snapping is a Post-Hoc Constraint:** The reported $0.019\text{ m}$ floor contact error is guaranteed by an explicit clamping heuristic (`_align_furniture_to_floor`), not raw monocular depth regression.
6. **Codebase & Test Rigor:** All 255 tests pass cleanly in 77.29s with zero regressions. Single-view 3D unprojection, floor-plane fitting, and bounding-box alignment are robust and production-ready for residential rooms.

### Detailed Findings per Sub-Gate:

### Sub-Gate A: Single-Image Metric-Depth Visualization — **GO**
- **Justification:** Powered by `Depth Anything V2 Metric Indoor Small` (24.8M params) running on CUDA FP32/FP16. Inference latency is $148\text{ ms}$ (depth only) and $1,211\text{ ms}$ (end-to-end scene unprojection). VRAM footprint is exceptionally modest at $317.9\text{ MB}$ ($96.1\%$ headroom on RTX 4060). Dense point cloud unprojection is visually cohesive, continuous, and free of spatial holes on residential drywall.
- **Decision:** **UNCONDITIONAL GO**. Fully ready for production and live demonstrations.

### Sub-Gate B: Furniture 3D Bounding-Box Estimation — **CONDITIONAL GO**
- **Justification:** Completely resolves the critical legacy bug where bounding boxes were disconnected at normalized $Z \approx 0.45\text{ m}$. Foreground depth clustering ($\le \mathrm{P}_{40} + 0.35\text{ m}$) reliably locks bounding boxes to the physical front surfaces of chairs, tables, and sofas.
- **Conditions for Full Certification:**
  1. Add semantic sanity envelopes (e.g. capping table heights to $\le 1.1\text{ m}$) to prevent absurd dimensions when detector false positives occur at extreme depths.
  2. Display an explicit disclaimer in the UI noting that physical dimensions are estimated under assumed camera focal lengths and are approximate.
- **Decision:** **CONDITIONAL GO**.

### Sub-Gate C: Room Structural Geometry — **CONDITIONAL GO**
- **Justification:** Planar RANSAC correctly extracts floor plane normal and camera height ($|n_y| \ge 0.70$) in standard residential rooms. Room bounding wireframes provide clear spatial context.
- **Conditions for Full Certification:**
  1. Restrict floor plane assumptions to residential indoor scenes; fail gracefully with clear user notification if non-planar outdoor or tilted environments are uploaded.
- **Decision:** **CONDITIONAL GO**.

### Sub-Gate D: Genuine Multi-View Registration — **NO-GO**
- **Justification:**
  1. The benchmark evaluated 2D cyclically shifted duplicates (`np.roll`), completely bypassing physical 3D multi-view baseline geometry.
  2. The mathematical formulation is rigid $\mathrm{SE}(3)$ with zero scale compensation ($\mathrm{Sim}(3)$), making it fragile against monocular depth scale drift between different perspectives.
  3. No real-world multi-camera benchmark dataset exists in the repository.
- **Decision:** **NO-GO**. Must not be advertised or deployed as validated multi-view registration.

### Sub-Gate E: Multi-View Fused Reconstruction — **NO-GO**
- **Justification:**
  1. Claims that multi-view fusion "revealed hidden dining chairs and uncovered occluded back walls" were physically impossible artifacts of evaluating 2D pixel-rolled arrays.
  2. The multi-view pipeline has never been tested against ground-truth multi-camera datasets (e.g. ScanNet, 7-Scenes, Replica).
- **Decision:** **NO-GO**. Requires genuine multi-view dataset collection and validation.

### Sub-Gate F: Overall Frontend Readiness — **CONDITIONAL GO**
- **Justification:**
  1. The Streamlit application and Plotly 3D viewer are stable, performant, and correctly handle single-view 3D scene unprojection, layout wireframes, oriented bounding boxes, and point-cloud export (PLY/GLB).
  2. The test suite passes 100% (255/255 tests green).
- **Conditions for UI / Showcase Deployment:**
  1. The single-view 3D reconstruction mode should be the primary, default product showcase.
  2. Any multi-view upload tab in the UI must be clearly marked as **"Experimental / Beta Preview"** with a tooltip stating that it currently supports narrow-baseline imagery.
- **Decision:** **CONDITIONAL GO**.

## 4. Auditor's Final Release Recommendation

The auditor recommends **approving RoomAI for Single-View 3D Scene Reconstruction demonstration and frontend presentation**, while **strictly withholding certification from the multi-view pipeline until genuine multi-camera data is benchmarked**.

In accordance with strict audit instructions:
- No frontend redesign has been initiated.
- No new features have been added.
- Existing production checkpoints and models have been strictly preserved.
- Phase 7.4.1 is hereby **CONCLUDED**.
