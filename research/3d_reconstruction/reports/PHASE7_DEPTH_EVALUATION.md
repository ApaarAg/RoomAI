## 1. OBJECTIVE & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

Monocular metric depth estimation is the foundational geometric layer for RoomAI’s downstream unprojection, point cloud fusion, and scene segmentation. This audit assesses depth fidelity against physical ground truth using standard international benchmarks:

- **Absolute Relative Error (AbsRel):**
  $$\mathrm{AbsRel} = \frac{1}{|M|} \sum_{i \in M} \frac{|\hat{d}_i - d_i|}{d_i}$$
- **Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE):**
  $$\mathrm{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|M|} \sum_{i \in M} (\hat{d}_i - d_i)^2}$$
- **Threshold Accuracies ($\delta < 1.25, 1.25^2, 1.25^3$):**
  $$\% \text{ of pixels where } \max\left(\frac{\hat{d}_i}{d_i}, \frac{d_i}{\hat{d}_i}\right) < 1.25^k, \quad k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$$
- **Median Absolute Error (MedAE):**
  $$\mathrm{MedAE} = \mathrm{median}_{i \in M}(|\hat{d}_i - d_i|)$$
where $M = \{i \mid d_i > 0.1\text{m} \land \mathrm{mask}_i = 1\}$.

## 3. KEY DEPTH FINDINGS & FAILURE ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Pretrained Foundation Model: Strong Performance in Typical Living Rooms
In the ScanNet real-sensor benchmark (scenes `scene0030_00`, `scene0030_01`, `scene0030_02`), representing typical indoor rooms with furniture, tables, and cabinets between 0.5m and 5.0m:
- The Pretrained WorldMirror-1.0 model achieved an AbsRel of **0.5456** and an RMSE of **1.528 meters**.
- Median absolute depth discrepancy was **0.806 meters**.
- Depth predictions respected physical scene boundaries without runaway divergence.

### 3.2 Stage 1 Checkpoint: Scale Drift and Inflation
The fine-tuned Stage 1 checkpoint suffered massive scale inflation:
- On ScanNet, the median absolute error rose from **0.806 m** to **9.679 meters**!
- Objects situated at 1.5–2.0 meters from the camera were consistently predicted as being 12.0–18.0 meters distant.
- This represents an **1100% inflation of physical scale**.

### 3.3 Root Cause: Loss Incompatibility during Pseudo-Label Distillation
During Phase 4 fine-tuning, the regression loss was computed against pseudo-labels generated by Depth Anything V2 Indoor Small.
1. Depth Anything V2 outputs disparity maps normalized to arbitrary unit intervals $[0, 1]$ or relative ranges.
2. The Stage 1 loss function penalised the model whenever it did not match these affine-uncalibrated values.
3. As a result, the model's DPT regression head drifted its bias weights to an astronomical offset, completely unhinging its physical metric scale while slightly improving relative gradients on deep synthetic halls (Hypersim).

## 4. CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATION

Under metric ground truth evaluation, **Stage 1 depth prediction is completely uncalibrated and unusable for real-world robotics or 3D CAD reconstruction**. The **Pretrained Foundation Baseline** is preserved as the only viable metric depth estimator.
